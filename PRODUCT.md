# DSR360 — B2B Industrial Lubricants Sales Operating System

## Product Overview
**DSR360** is a field-grade AI-powered Sales Operating System designed specifically for B2B Direct Sales Representatives (DSR) distributing **Shell Commercial Lubricants** in Indonesia.

## Target Audience & Usage Context
- **User**: Field Sales Engineers & Account Managers (Bang Radit / Shell DSR).
- **Environment**: Factory floors, mining sites, palm oil mills (PKS), fleet depots, and mobile transit.
- **Device**: Mobile first (smartphones 375px–430px in portrait via PWA), tablets, and desktop workstations.
- **Key Challenges Solved**: Heavy sunlight glare, oily fingers, rapid client meetings, multi-tiered price negotiations, technical spec consultations across 443 SKUs, and post-visit administrative burden.

## Core Capabilities
1. **☀️ Morning Tactical Briefing & Target Quota Meters**: AI-driven daily battle plan grounded in the 13 Masterpiece Sales Frameworks.
2. **🏢 Customer 360° Dossier & Machine Fleet Profiling**: Complete telemetry on customer machinery, competitor oil displacement, volume run rates, and key decision makers.
3. **📋 POPSA-Grounded Visit Planner & 10-Second Debrief**: Structured pre-call objectives and instant audio/text debriefs updating client intelligence.
4. **💼 Multi-Product Deal Pipeline & Volume Forecasting**: Real-time tracking of deal stages, liter volumes, total contract value, and win probabilities.
5. **🎯 AI Daily Follow-Up Cockpit & Radar**: Predictive alerts for accounts entering buying cycles, churn risks, and overdue touchpoints.
6. **🌙 Hermes Nightly Forensic Worker**: Autonomous scanner identifying cross-sell opportunities, competitor displacement risks, and automated WA drafts.
7. **📖 Shell Technical Hub (443 SKUs)**: Instant searchable technical specifications, ISO VG grades, OEM approvals, and 1-click WhatsApp quotes.
